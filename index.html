Our mission

Women deserve better options.

Too many women are offered treatments that were never designed with them in mind. We're building a new approach, grounded in academic research and shaped by the surgeons who care for these patients every day.

Restore, don't replace

We focus on helping the body rebuild healthy tissue, rather than relying on permanent fixes.

Built with clinicians

Leading surgeons help shape our work from the start, so it fits the way care is actually delivered.

Grounded in research

Our work began at Dartmouth and is supported by non-dilutive federal research funding.
